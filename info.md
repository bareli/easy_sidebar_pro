# Easy Sidebar Pro

Collapsible groups for the Home Assistant sidebar, edited inside the sidebar with drag and drop.
Drop one item onto another to make a group. Group colours and header styles, groups that start
collapsed or open one at a time, and a compact grid of pinned icons at the bottom. Per-user layouts
stored in Home Assistant, admin default, Hebrew and English.

After installing: add the integration, then refresh the browser and use the pencil next to the
sidebar title.
