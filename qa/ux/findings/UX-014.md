## Summary

At 320x568 a tabbed group that holds a Home Assistant config page (a link to /config/automation or /config/devices) shows two bottom tab bars: HA's own (white, icon + text) and ours (grey pills) directly under it. 104 px of 568 are bars plus the 56 px header, about 408 px left for the page. Both look like tab bars, so a non-technical user cannot tell which one changes what. Our bar also cuts its fourth tab ("פעילות") at the screen edge with no fade, so nothing hints that it scrolls.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Layout: tabs group with links /config/automation/dashboard, /config/devices/dashboard, History, a web link.
2. Phone 320x568 touch, open /config/automation/dashboard.

## Expected

Visually distinct bars, or no group bar while the page brings its own bottom tabs; an edge fade or arrow on the scrolling bar.

## Actual

Measured: strip top 520, height 48 on both config pages, HA tabs directly above.
