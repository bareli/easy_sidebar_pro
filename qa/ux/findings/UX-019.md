## Summary

In the icon-only rail HA's own rows get `ha-tooltip`; our group row only sets `title` (browser tooltip after about 1 s, never on touch). Long mixed names (50 chars: Hebrew, English, emoji) are cut with an ellipsis in the expanded sidebar, sometimes leaving a word fragment ("…t Home"); the full name is only in aria-label (HA's own long titles are cut the same way). Emoji, ZWJ sequences, flags and the 50-char limit all rendered and saved fine; a 60-char paste is cut to 50 without a message.

## Steps to reproduce

Layout with a 50-char group name; collapse to the rail; hover the group.

## Expected

ha-tooltip on groups as on other rows.

## Actual

Native `title` only (measured).
