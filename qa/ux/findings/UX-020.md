## Summary

Search works well: "his" finds History in the Hebrew UI (via the untranslated title), "משחק" finds the view "משחקים · חדר ילדים", "גינה" finds "גינה אחורית · מצלמות". But a family member will never find the switch: it is the fifth checkbox inside the collapsed Display options and it is the owner's setting. HA's Settings sub-pages are not searchable ("אוטומ" finds nothing without a link).

## Steps to reproduce

Editor, Display options, Search box (off).

## Expected

Mention it in the editor hint or first-run message; consider on by default with 15+ panels.

## Actual

Off by default.
