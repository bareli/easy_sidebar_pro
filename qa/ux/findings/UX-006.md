## Problem

Each item is small. Together they are the difference between "plugin" and "feels native".

1. **Long English titles in Hebrew show their end, not their start.** "Energy and solar overview
   for the whole house" appears in the editor as "...or the whole house" (the row cannot be
   identified). HA's native row does the same ("...erview for the whole house"), but the editor
   owns its own row: `dir="auto"` on `.title` would show "Energy and solar ov...".
2. **Map icon differs** between the sidebar (account-pin icon, HA's own) and the editor (`mdi:map`
   from `panel.icon`). Users match rows by icon while dragging.
3. **Disabled eye on the default dashboard has no reason.** HA's dialog labels it "סקירה כללית
   (ברירת מחדל)". Ours shows a faded eye whose tooltip still says "הסתרת סקירה כללית".
4. *(Moved: duplicate of BUG-005, filed there by the accessibility review.)*
5. **Plural / joining in strings (source-level, screen-reader only):** `items` "{n} items" / "{n}
   פריטים" gives "1 items" / "1 פריטים" for a one-panel group. `grouped` "נוצרה קבוצה עם {a}
   ו{b}" gives "וGarden" before an English name; use "ועם {b}" or "{a}, {b}".
6. **Admin sees "Reset to the default layout"** right after publishing their own layout as the
   default, where it does nothing useful. Hide Reset when the user's layout equals the default.
7. **Icon chips:** 16 chips in a 5-column grid leave one orphan on the last row; no TV, kids,
   lights-on or water icons, which are common Hebrew-home groups. A searchable picker is the real
   fix (SPEC notes HA's picker failed in the sidebar). Typed `mdi:` names are a tinkerer feature,
   not a family one.
8. **Ghost overflows** the sidebar edge by 12px toward the content (RTL) during a drag.
9. **"New group" duplicates:** "Add group" twice gives two groups called "קבוצה חדשה". Numbering
   ("קבוצה חדשה 2") avoids confusion if the user skips naming.

## Why it matters

None blocks a task. 1, 2 and 3 cost a moment of "which row is that?" during every edit session for
a Hebrew user with English dashboard names, which is the owner's real setup.
