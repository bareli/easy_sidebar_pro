## Problem

"Drop onto a row = make a group" is the product's signature gesture, and the only cue that you are
about to do it is an outline and tint on the target row. The ghost (a 92% opaque copy of the dragged
row, positioned so the pointer keeps its grab offset) sits **exactly over the row under the
pointer**, which is the target. The result:

- "before / after": the 3px line peeks out above or below the ghost, so it is visible.
- "onto" (merge): the outline is under the ghost. Only a sliver of the outline shows at the far edge.
  The target row's name is hidden too, so the user cannot see which row they are about to group with.

There is also no wording during the drag. Nothing says "create a group with מפה". The two
outcomes (move vs create group) differ only by an outline the user cannot see.

## Why it matters

Brief question: "does a new user discover drop onto a row = group?" The hint text tells them it
exists, but when they try it, the screen gives no confirmation that they are in the right zone. They
learn it by accident, after the drop (the name field appears). Mis-drops are cheap to undo, but the
first-run experience of the headline feature is guesswork.

## Evidence

![before line visible, onto outline hidden under the ghost, result](https://github.com/bareli/easy_sidebar_pro/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-003-onto-outline-under-ghost.png?raw=1)

Left: pointer in the top 30% of "מפה" (line visible above the ghost). Middle: pointer in the middle
of "מפה". The `drop-into` class is set on that row, but the ghost covers it; only a 4px blue edge
shows at the left. Right: after the drop, the group exists with the name field selected (good).

## Recommendation

1. Move the ghost off the target. Either offset it (for example 24px toward inline-end and half a row
   down), or shrink it to a compact chip (icon + name) beside the pointer.
2. During an "onto" hover, change the target row itself: stronger tint, and an inline label on that
   row, "קבוצה חדשה עם מפה" / "New group with Map", replacing its eye button.
3. Optional: a short hold (about 300ms) on the middle zone before "onto" activates, so passing over
   rows while reordering does not flicker into group mode.
