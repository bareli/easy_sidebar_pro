## Summary

Rules live inside the closed ⋮ / More block. A row with a rule looks identical to one without, in the editor, and when the rule hides it the group is simply absent from the sidebar. A household member cannot tell why the "Alarm" group is missing; the person who set it up months ago has forgotten.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Group with badge counter.open_windows and show-only-when input_boolean.alarm, alarm off.
2. Sidebar: group gone. Open editor: group row looks plain.

## Expected

A small indicator on rows with a rule and a tooltip ("Shown only while Alarm is on"); optionally dim the row while its rule hides it.

## Actual

No indicator; every row's options must be opened to audit.
