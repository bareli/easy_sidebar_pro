**WCAG 2.1 AA: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum).** Pre-existing (3.31:1 before the #43 fix, 3.12:1 after; reported by the developer, confirmed here).

## Summary

`.btn.danger` is white (#fff) 14 px text on `--esp-error-color`. Light theme: 6.91:1 (pass). Dark theme: error colour rgb(221,115,106), white text = **3.12:1**, below 4.5:1. Because the error colour was lifted in dark for text on dark panels, it is too light for white text on top of it.

## Steps to reproduce

1. 8173, dark theme (`frontend/set_user_data` key `theme` = `{dark:true}`), open the editor, click "Clear default" (admin) in the footer.
2. Read `getComputedStyle(.btn.danger)` colour and background.

## Expected

4.5:1 for 14 px normal-weight text.

## Actual

Dark: fg rgb(255,255,255) on rgb(221,115,106) = 3.12:1. Light: 6.91:1.

## Evidence

`~/.claude/qa-playwright/espjs/ver-a11y-1.mjs` (DARK=1, PORT=8173): `DANGER {"cr":3.12,...}`; light `cr 6.91`.

![Danger button in light theme, 6.91:1](https://github.com/bareli/easy_sidebar_pro/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-024-danger-button-light.png?raw=1)

## Relevant files

- `custom_components/easy_sidebar_pro/www/easy-sidebar-pro.js` EDITOR_CSS line ~670 `.btn.danger`

## Recommended remediation

Use dark text on the light error fill in dark theme (for example the sidebar background colour), or a separate darker background token for the danger button; keep white-on-error in light.
