## Summary

Standard: WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA). Measured from computed colours (alpha composited over
the real background), default HA theme, 14 px regular unless stated:

| Element | Light | Dark | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| "סיום" (Done): white on `--primary-color` #009ac7 | 3.26 | 3.26 | 4.5 |
| "הוספת קבוצה" and footer links, primary-colour text | 3.26 | 5.23 (pass) | 4.5 |
| Error text (12 px, #db4437) | 4.29 | 3.97 | 4.5 |
| Title of a hidden panel (opacity .45) | 3.00 | 3.68 | 4.5 (see note) |

Everything else measured passes: row titles 13-15:1, secondary text and icons 5.2-6.5:1, the pencil 4.29 (a graphic, needs 3:1).
Note on hidden titles: dimmed on purpose (state is also shown by the eye-off icon and the label); if the product
treats them as an inactive control this row may be exempt. The other rows are not. The colours are HA's defaults and
a theme can change them, but the product ships on the default.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the editor (mouse), hide one panel with its eye button, open a group's icon editor, enter "foo", Enter.
2. Read computed colours of `.btn.primary`, `.add`, `.link`, `.error`, `.row.hidden .title` and composite over their background.
3. Repeat in the HA dark theme (localStorage `selectedTheme` = {"dark":true}, reload).

## Expected

4.5:1 for text below 24 px regular / 18.66 px bold (SC 1.4.3).

## Actual

See the table.

## Evidence

Dark run output: `Done 3.26 fg=255,255,255 bg=0,154,199`, `icon error 3.97 fg=219,68,55 bg=28,28,28 12px`,
`HIDDEN row title 3.68`. Light values were computed from the logged colours (error 4.29, add and links 3.26, hidden 3.00).
Measurement script is in the run scratchpad (`qa-a11y/contrast2.mjs`), not committed.

## Screenshots

![editor in light theme: Done, Add group, red error, dimmed hidden title](https://github.com/bareli/easy_sidebar_pro/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-006-editor-contrast-light.png?raw=1)

## Likely root cause

Likely: colours taken straight from theme variables (`--primary-color`, `--error-color`, opacity .45) with no pair check.

## Relevant files

`custom_components/easy_sidebar_pro/www/easy-sidebar-pro.js` (`EDITOR_CSS`: `.btn.primary`, `.add`, `.link`, `.error`, `.row.hidden`).

## Recommended remediation

A design call, hence model:opus: link-style actions in `--primary-text-color` with an underline or icon; darker or bolder
error text; a darker Done fill or dark text on it; hidden titles at higher opacity with the eye-off icon as the cue.
Re-measure in light and dark.

## Regression test

A11Y-04: contrast pairs in both themes. A browser measurement; a CSS-text test can only pin the chosen values, and the doc comment must say so.
