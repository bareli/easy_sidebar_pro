**WCAG 2.1 AA: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast.**

## Summary

The v0.5 badges (count pills on rows and groups, and the "active" dot) are drawn with HA's `.badge` styling: white text on `rgb(255,152,0)`. That is 2.16:1 in both light and dark themes (the pill is the same colour in both). A count (14 px / 12 px, regular weight) needs 4.5:1; the dot, the only visual carrier of "active", needs 3:1 against the sidebar: on the light sidebar (white) it is 2.16:1. Low-vision users cannot read the number or see the dot. The names exposed to screen readers are correct (count or "פעיל" once).

## Steps to reproduce

1. On 8173 as qa_admin set a layout with `items`: `history: {badge: "sensor.qa_count"}` (state 5), `logbook: {badge: "binary_sensor.qa_dot"}` (state on), and a badge on group `g:a`.
2. Load the page in light (and dark) theme; read the computed colours of the `.badge` spans on rows and in `esp-group`.

## Expected

4.5:1 for the count text, 3:1 for the dot against the sidebar background (WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard declared in qa/PROJECT.md).

## Actual

| Element | fg | bg | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| row count "5" (14 px) | 255,255,255 | 255,152,0 | **2.16:1** (light and dark) |
| group badge "5" (12 px) | 255,255,255 | 255,152,0 | **2.16:1** |
| dot vs white sidebar | 255,152,0 | 255,255,255 | **2.16:1** (dark sidebar 28,28,28: passes) |

HA's own notification badges ("3", "1" on Settings and Notifications) use the same colours, so the value is inherited from HA's default theme; the feature still ships it for content it adds.

## Evidence

Measured in-page (canvas-resolved colours, WCAG formula): `~/.claude/qa-playwright/espjs/a11y-5.mjs` (PORT=8173, DARK unset / 1).

## Screenshots

## Likely root cause

Likely: the colours come from HA's `.badge` rule (`--accent-color` background, white text) and the integration does not override them (as it did for the primary colour in BUG-006). What would prove it: override with a darker mix and re-measure.

## Relevant files

- `custom_components/easy_sidebar_pro/www/easy-sidebar-pro.js` (badge spans near line 412; `esp-group` `.badge`)

## Recommended remediation

For our own badges derive a background that gives 4.5:1 with the text (or use the theme text colour on a tinted pill), and give the dot a darker fill or an outline reaching 3:1 against the sidebar, in light and dark.
